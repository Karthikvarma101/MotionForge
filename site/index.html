Motion graphics,
native to your Mac.

MotionForge is a fast, native animation and compositing app for Apple silicon — keyframes, text animators, effects, masks, audio and hardware-encoded export, built on Metal.

Download for Mac View the source
Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or later) · macOS 26 or later · free to edit, Pro to export

Everything a motion piece needs

Built from the ground up for the GPU in your Mac, so previews play in real time and exports use the hardware encoders.

Layers that move

Shapes, images, text, pen-tool paths, nulls and parenting, precomps with time remapping.

Animation tools

Keyframes with easing, bezier motion paths, a graph editor with speed handles and ready-made presets.

Text animators

Animate characters, words or lines — typewriters, staggers and pop-ins with range selectors.

Effects and mattes

GPU blur, glow, drop shadow and colour; masks, track mattes, blend modes and motion blur.

Audio

Voice-overs and music on the timeline with waveforms and animatable levels, mixed into every export.

Export

H.264, HEVC, ProRes 422 / 4444 with alpha or PNG sequences, rendered in the background while you keep working.

Precise when you need it

Shape every move in the graph editor, scrub any value, and see exactly what the renderer is doing — GPU time, frame cache and preview resolution are always on screen.

Pricing

Edit, preview and save for free. Every new account gets 1 week of Pro free — after that, MotionForge Pro adds exporting. Payments by Razorpay (UPI autopay or card); cancel any time.
