- Cache hit (evaluate + lookup, 1,000 layers): median 258.6 µs, p95 269.3 µs
- Dependency-based invalidation: recolouring 1 layer (visible 0–2 s) out of 1,000 changed 60 of 300 frame hashes (expected 60): only frames whose evaluated result changed are re-rendered
- Frame-cache footprint (8-bit premultiplied copies): 7.9 MB per 1080p frame, 31.6 MB per 4K frame — 4 s of 1080p30 per GB
- Thermal state at end: nominal

Throughput = frames / wall-clock time with up to 3 frames in flight; it is bounded by whichever of CPU evaluate+encode or GPU execution is slower. These are renderer-only numbers (no viewer presentation, no media decode).

Note on run-to-run variance: absolute GPU times on this machine drift with recent load (the same
5,000-shape frames measured 13.1 / 13.9 / 15.1 ms in three consecutive interleaved runs). Compare
builds with `benchmarks/ab/run.sh`, not with numbers from different runs.
